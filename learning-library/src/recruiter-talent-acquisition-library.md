# From zero to first recruiter role: a roadmap-ordered learning library

> [!NOTE] Read this first
> Built on 26 October 2026 for an absolute beginner in Lagos, Nigeria, who wants to enter HR through the recruiter and talent acquisition path, with about five study hours a week. Work through the stages in order. Research began on that date, with selected later checks noted on the cards. Some access findings remain partial, conditional or unconfirmed; dates are not promises of current terms. Terms change; re-check before you pay or enrol. This is a learning plan. It cannot promise a job, a professional credential or mastery.

## Start here

This library has 8 stages and 20 resource cards, with 94 planned study hours across 20 weeks. Study budgets include practice and selected lessons, not every full course. Free study, certificates and professional qualifications are separate findings.

The most useful idea in the whole plan sits in Stage 6: ask every candidate the same well-designed questions and score the answers against a rubric you wrote before you met them. Stages 1 to 5 exist so that you can do that well. Stage 7 keeps you on the right side of Nigerian law. Stage 8 turns the practice into evidence you can show an employer.

Every resource retained in the active roadmap has a confirmed free-study route. Accounts may still be needed, optional certificates may cost extra, and software trials mentioned alongside public samples are not required to study those samples. Paid or conditional products discussed below are not active learning cards. None of the free study or certificates here is a professional HR qualification.

## The stages and their resources

## Stage 1: Orientation - what a recruiter actually does

Learn the hiring lifecycle end to end, the vocabulary of talent acquisition, and where the recruiter fits alongside hiring managers. No prior HR knowledge assumed.

**Level:** Absolute beginner; no prerequisites.

**Effort:** about 8 hours across weeks 1-2. This includes a one-hour HR Essentials starter budget; use selected lessons, not both full playlists.

**Outcomes:**

- Describe the stages from workforce planning to onboarding
- Name the common recruiter roles and the difference between agency and in-house recruiting
- Explain why hiring decisions should be based on defined criteria rather than gut feel

**Practice task:** Draw the hiring lifecycle from workforce planning to onboarding and label who owns each step.

**Resources in this stage:** 2.

## Stage 2: Foundations - fair process, job roles and the law

Build the ground rules: equal opportunity, job and person requirements, the recruitment and selection sequence, and the legal duties that apply when handling candidate information in Nigeria.

**Level:** Beginner; Stage 1 vocabulary assumed.

**Effort:** about 14 hours across weeks 3-5. The 14-hour figure is a planning allowance for the selected OpenLearn sections, Saylor Units 2–3 and practice.

**Outcomes:**

- Run a full and fair recruitment and selection process step by step
- Separate job requirements from person specifications
- Recognise the basic compliance duties that apply to candidate data in Nigeria

**Practice task:** Write a one-page fair-process checklist for a small Lagos employer hiring its first employee.

**Resources in this stage:** 2.

## Stage 3: Define the role - job analysis, descriptions and success criteria

Before sourcing anyone, learn to work out what the job actually requires and what good evidence of it looks like. Most bad hiring starts here.

**Level:** Beginner; Stages 1 and 2 assumed.

**Effort:** about 10 hours across weeks 6-7.

**Outcomes:**

- Turn a vague request into a written job description and person specification
- Define measurable hiring attributes before speaking to candidates
- Build a simple scorecard that every interviewer can use

**Practice task:** Produce a job description, a person specification and a six-attribute scorecard for one real vacancy.

**Resources in this stage:** 3.

## Stage 5: Screen and shortlist - reading CVs against criteria

Turn a pile of applications into a defensible shortlist. Practice consistent screening before you ever speak to a candidate.

**Level:** Beginner to intermediate; a scorecard from Stage 3 assumed.

**Effort:** about 10 hours across weeks 11-12.

- Screen a CV against the scorecard instead of against your impressions
- Write a short phone-screen script with the same questions for everyone

## Stage 6: Interview and decide - structured interviewing in practice

Learn the highest-value skill in this roadmap: asking the same well-designed questions and scoring answers against a written rubric.

**Level:** Intermediate; a scorecard and interview questions assumed.

**Effort:** about 16 hours across weeks 13-15.

**Outcomes:**

- Write behavioural and hypothetical questions tied to defined attributes
- Build a rubric that distinguishes poor, borderline, solid and outstanding answers
- Run a mock interview and score it independently of a partner

**Practice task:** Run a 30-minute mock interview with a friend, score it independently, then compare your scores.

**Resources in this stage:** 2.

## Stage 7: Offer, onboarding and the law in Nigeria

Close the loop: references, offers, onboarding, and the two legal areas that a Lagos-based recruiter will meet immediately - data protection and employment law.

**Level:** Intermediate; the full hiring process from Stages 1 to 6 assumed.

**Effort:** about 10 hours across weeks 16-17.

**Outcomes:**

- Explain the lawful bases for processing candidate data under Nigerian law
- Draft a short candidate privacy notice
- Read the recruiting provisions of the Labour Act and know when to escalate to a lawyer

**Practice task:** Draft a candidate privacy notice and a 30-day onboarding checklist.

**Resources in this stage:** 3.

### Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025

**Provider:** Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

**Link:** https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf

**Format:** Free primary legal guidance (official PDF)

**Cost:** Free

**Time:** About 3 hours for the relevant articles

**Why:** A recruiter handles CVs, references and background checks, which are personal data. The GAID sets out the principles, lawful bases, consent rules, information duties for data subjects, breach notification and data processing agreements.

**Do this:** Read Articles 15, 16, 17, 18, 27, 33 and 34, then draft a six-line privacy notice you could put on a job application form.

**Access and verification (2026-10-26):** Checked 2026-10-26 by opening the official NDPC PDF: the document is publicly readable without an account, form, trial or payment; no certificate is offered. I checked its arrangement of articles and opening provisions, not every assigned article or the whole directive. It is regulatory guidance, not legal advice; confirm current law with a Nigerian lawyer.

### Labour Act, Chapter L1 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004

**Provider:** PLAC Laws of Nigeria (statute copy); ILO NATLEX listing

**Link:** https://lawsofnigeria.placng.org/laws/L1.pdf

**Format:** Free primary legislation (official-style PDF)

**Cost:** Free

**Time:** About 2 hours for Parts I and II

**Why:** Part II is specifically about recruiting, including prohibitions on recruiting without a permit or licence, employer permits, recruiter licences and restrictions. Section 7 covers written particulars of employment terms, which matters when you prepare an offer.

**Do this:** Read sections 7, 23, 24, 25, 26 and 71. Write down three questions you would ask an employer before running a search on their behalf.

**Access and verification (2026-10-26):** Checked 2026-10-26 by opening the public PLAC PDF: no account, form, trial or payment is required; no certificate is offered. This is an official-style reproduction, not a government gazette. The Labour Act dates from 1971/Cap. L1 LFN 2004 and may have been amended or partly superseded; verify current law and seek Nigerian legal advice before acting.

## What was excluded, and why

- **University of Minnesota, Recruiting, Hiring, and Onboarding Employees (Coursera) — removed from Stage 2.** Full-course access was not confirmed free. Although the course page said “Enroll for free”, its FAQ tied course-material access to paid Certificate access, a temporary free trial, or Full Course, No Certificate where available. Coursera Help says previews for most courses are limited to the first module with no certificate and the no-certificate route is available only for selected courses; I could not confirm it for this course. A Coursera account may be required for enrollment or preview, and no account-free full-course route was verified. The course-specific trial duration, post-trial billing/cancellation terms and Nigeria checkout price remain unverified. Financial aid is mentioned, but course-specific coverage and eligibility were not confirmed. It is excluded from active study; the course and help-page findings remain in the verification manifest.
- **Simplilearn SkillUp, LearnTube.ai, LearnCafe and similar "free HR certificate" listings.** Their free-certificate claims came from roundup articles rather than provider pages. One provider page stated that the course was available to subscribers. I could not confirm that the full learning material opens without payment, so they do not count as free here.
- **SHRM, CIPD and HRCI member toolkits.** Their public articles are useful, but the templates and exam preparation sit behind membership or a paid assessment. Excluded from the free list; no judgement on their quality.
- **The long directories of "best recruiter YouTube channels".** Only HR Learning Lab was verified directly. I did not add the other channels, because a list of unverified links would pad the library without teaching anything.
- **Free marketing and sales certifications.** HubSpot Academy is genuinely free, but a full marketing certification would pull you away from the recruiter path. Only its short CRM lesson appears, in Stage 8, where a candidate tracker is actually needed.
- **Unverified core access.** A provider index or “free” headline was not enough when the assigned learning material or practice route could not be verified. Jobberman’s free Basic listing requires Customer Experience confirmation, and a draft-only practice flow could not be confirmed; it is not retained.
- **Google re:Work Review resumes.** The index lists the guide, but the English direct page returned 404; it is not counted as a usable free study resource.
- **Greenhouse Stage 5 guidance hub.** Some relevant linked pages show contact-form gates; free access to the assigned screening/bias material was not established, so that card is not retained.

There are also two gaps worth naming. Free, current, Nigeria-specific recruiter training is scarce; the local legal material here is primary legislation and regulator guidance, which is harder to read than a course but more reliable. And several core guides come from US employers or US government data. They teach a method that transfers, but their examples, job titles and legal assumptions do not. Stage 7 exists to correct that.

## About the interactive tracker

The single-file app stores your progress in this browser's local storage and counts a streak using the device's local calendar. It checks reminders only while the app page is open. It cannot send background notifications, does not sync between devices, and has no backend or push service. Browser notifications need a supported browser, your permission and a secure context such as HTTPS or localhost; if they are unavailable or denied, the in-app check-in still works. Use the export button now and then to keep a manual backup, and import that file if you change devices.
